What You'll Learn

As AI tools become part of everyday clinical and administrative work, social workers need more than good intentions — they need a practical framework for deciding when and how to use these tools responsibly. This workshop builds that framework from the ground up: a quick everyday gut-check, a structured approach for harder calls, real case studies of what happens when it goes wrong, and a concrete plan for bringing responsible AI use back to your agency or practice.

Learning Objectives

  • Build a Practical Decision Framework: Learn a quick, repeatable gut-check for everyday AI decisions in practice.
  • Ground Your Judgment in Professional Ethics: Apply the NASW Code of Ethics and a structured ethical decision model to harder, high-stakes calls.
  • Write Prompts as a Clinical Skill: Learn to write AI prompts that produce safe, usable, client-ready output.
  • Learn from Real Case Studies: Work through real-world-pattern cases illustrating what can go wrong when AI is used without adequate safeguards.
  • Evaluate and Select Tools Safely: Use a comparison guide and compliance checklist to evaluate AI tools before adopting them.
  • Take Home an Implementation Roadmap: Leave with a roadmap and policy template for your agency or practice.
