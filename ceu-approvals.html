How CE Program Approval Works

As a CE approval organization authorized by LABSWE, Greenpath Wellness, LLC can review your continuing education program and, if it meets LABSWE-aligned standards, issue an approval that allows you to offer it for Louisiana social work CE credit. This is a good fit whether you're an individual presenter, an agency, or an organization that isn't itself an authorized LABSWE CE approval organization.

What We Look For

We score every application using the same Guide for Assessment of Continuing Education that LABSWE uses to evaluate CE offerings. Programs are scored across three categories below; a combined total of 10 or more is required for approval, and a score of zero in any single category disqualifies the program, regardless of total score.

Program Content
6Mainstream social work knowledge, skills, and values
6Specialized social work knowledge, skills, and values
4Information from related fields that is useful for social work practice
2Developing areas that may lack strong research, support, or clear application
0Content that is specifically not acceptable or not related to social work practice
Program Presenter
5Social worker with appropriate expertise in the content area
4Related professional with the ability to connect content to social work practice
2Lay person (e.g., a client) speaking to the impact of needing/receiving services
0Presenter with no apparent professional qualifications or link to social work practice
Program Audience
4Social work practitioners/students
4Interdisciplinary professional audience that may include social workers
3Audience presumed to be primarily from another profession (e.g., nursing)
1Audience open to the general public
0Audience presumed to be primarily the general public
Passing score: Total of 10+ across all three categories combined, with no category scoring zero. (Source: LABSWE's Guide for Assessment of Continuing Education.)

Two additional requirements apply outside of this scoring guide:

  • Social Worker Involvement: A credentialed or licensed social worker is involved as a consultant, planner, presenter, or reviewer for the program.
  • Clear Objectives & Evaluation: The program has defined learning objectives, a timed agenda, and an evaluation tool for participant feedback.

What You'll Need to Submit

  • Program title, date, and format (in-person location or live virtual)
  • Sponsoring organization or individual contact information
  • Instructor bio or CV for each presenter
  • Detailed agenda/outline with learning objectives and a time table
  • Name and credentials of the social worker involved in planning, presenting, or reviewing the program
  • Requested CE hours by category (Clinical, Ethics, Supervision, and/or General)
  • Promotional flyer or brochure, if available
  • A blank copy of the evaluation tool you'll use with participants
  • Confirmation of your complaint/refund procedure and ADA accommodation policy
  • Name and contact information for whoever will maintain attendance records (required to be kept for 3 years)

Approval Process & Timeline

  1. Submit Your Application: Complete the form below and attach whatever supporting materials you have available. You can also email additional documents afterward.
  2. Completeness Review: We'll confirm your application is complete. Incomplete applications can't move forward until missing information is provided.
  3. Invoice & Fee: Once your application is confirmed complete, we'll send an invoice for the applicable fee. Payment is due before an approval determination is issued.
  4. Program Review: Your program is evaluated against LABSWE-aligned criteria for content, presenter qualifications, and audience.
  5. Approval Determination: You'll receive a written approval determination by email, typically within 10 business days of a complete, paid application. We recommend applying at least 4–6 weeks before your program date.
